# USS *Endeavor*
 
**A cozy, episodic space adventure — Season One**
 
**[▶ Play now](https://donovanschell.github.io/uss-endeavor-game/)** — no install, no account, just open and go.
 
You're the captain of a small exploration vessel, months out on deployment. Across nine episodes, you'll make first contact, navigate diplomatic incidents no manual prepared you for, wake something on a derelict ship that shouldn't still be running, fly into a region of space where the instruments can't agree with each other, and eventually — finally — bring your crew home.
 
There's no combat score, no fail states that end your run, and no correct answers waiting to be found. Just a ship, a crew, and a season's worth of choices that actually stick.
 
## What's in the season
 
- **9 full episodes, 34 chapters** — a complete, playable story from first contact through the long way home
- **8 senior officers**, each with their own independent relationship track that responds to how you actually play, not just what you say
- **Ship systems** — Hull, Power, and Morale respond to your choices across the season; alert conditions (Green, Yellow, Red, Blue, Silent Running, Gray) shift with the story
- **Daily activities** — stabilize the core, run diagnostics, reroute power, and more, scattered across the ship's decks
- **A flight deck** — real piloting objectives, from diplomatic courier runs to precision docking
- **Everything's local** — progress saves automatically in your browser; nothing leaves your device
## How to play
 
Just open `index.html` in any modern browser — or if you're reading this on the live GitHub Pages link, you're already there. No install, no account, nothing to configure. Pick a ship name, pick a captain name, and go.
 
Progress saves automatically as you play. Clearing your browser's site data for this page will also clear your save.
